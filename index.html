Plant of the Week

Burning Bush

Not everything in the garden is for pollinators. The Burning Bush is cultivated primarily for it’s ornamental qualities. It’s leaves turn a stunning crimson colour in the fall. This particular specimen is over 28 years old and will not increase further in size. It serves as a popular roost and food source for cardinals and other song birds. For more information see here.
