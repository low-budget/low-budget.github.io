Plant of the Week

New England aster

The New England aster is a native Ontario perennial species with showy, bright, rose-purple flowers with orange-yellow centres which bloom in profusion at the tips of the leafy branches. They grow between 60 to 150 cm tall and adapt well to full sun or partial shade. They provide critical late-season food for bees and monarch butterflies.
